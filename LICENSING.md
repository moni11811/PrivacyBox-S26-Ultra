# Licensing model

## Selected model

Project-authored source code is offered under **GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later**. Contributions are accepted only with:

1. the project Contributor License Agreement in [CLA.md](CLA.md); and
2. a Developer Certificate of Origin (`Signed-off-by`) statement on every commit.

This is the strongest practical open-source structure for the stated goal:

- AGPL requires distributors of modified versions to provide corresponding source, and its section 13 also covers modified versions operated for users over a network.
- The CLA gives the named project owner a perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable copyright and patent license to accepted contributions, including the rights needed to maintain, distribute, sublicense, and relicense the project.
- DCO sign-off records that each contributor had the right to submit the contribution under the project's terms.

## What this cannot guarantee

No repository license can give the project owner permanent access to every future program that independently calls Samsung's API. Copyright licenses bind copies and derivatives of this project; they do not create ownership of Samsung's API, bind people who never accept the project's terms, or force disclosure of a clean-room independent implementation. The AGPL also permits genuinely private modifications that are neither conveyed nor offered as a network service to outside users.

The CLA binds only contributors whose contributions the project accepts under that agreement. It is not a copyright assignment: contributors retain ownership while granting the project broad, durable rights.
